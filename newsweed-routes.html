What the Newsweed mat is

  • One printed sheet, full color, that works as a table mat in diners and coffee shops.
  • One great picture every week.
  • Local sports, dances and clubs, music, crime, corruption, national news and entertainment.
  • Every story has a QR code: a short summary, then Newsweed’s videos, free classifieds and the full story.
  • Printed and dated: once it is out, nobody can change it.

For shops and businesses

  • Put the mats on your tables: your shop gets a month of online advertising in trade.
  • Advertise on the mat: your ad sits in front of customers for a whole meal, next to your neighbors’. Online ads start now at AdHotBox.
