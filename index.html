What makes this one different
  • Every seller was telephoned before their first listing.
  • Name, telephone and location on file — nomads welcome, with location on.
  • Flat fees, never a cut of the price. The seller’s price is the seller’s.
  • Money is held until the work is done. Nothing is deleted — the record is the asset.
If you need someone

Post it, read the bids, pick a name.

  1. Say what you need, where (remote or at your place), and what you would pay.
  2. Verified sellers bid. You see their name, their town, their ratings, their dated work.
  3. Pay Gigapoo; we hold it. Two days after delivery it goes to the seller.

Your side of the fee is flat: $10 on a sale. Work at your address needs your name and telephone too — the same rule both ways.

If you can do something

Say what you do, name your price, get called.

  1. Your name, telephone, a photo, where you are. Nomad? Keep location on.
  2. A person telephones you. After that, you can list and bid on every site the engine runs.
  3. Your price is paid in full to you; the buyer pays the flat fee on top.

Your side of the fee is flat: $25 on a written job, more for voice or a licensed opinion. Never a percentage.

Nobody is anonymous.Sellers and in-place buyers: real name, real telephone, a location, a call. Enforced by the engine, not written on a page.
You say what you can do.No list of categories. Sellers describe the work in their own words at their own price; the market groups what people offer.
Any task except illegal or dangerous.A short published refusal list. Everything else is allowed. Flags are reviewed; posts are not pre-screened.
Opinion is not advice.Work is a named person’s opinion unless the seller holds a licence on file — then, and only then, it may be marked advice.
Money is held.The buyer pays Gigapoo. Two days after delivery, the seller is paid. In-place work: the address is confirmed and both sides can see the meeting record.
Nothing is deleted.Work, ratings and disputes are dated and kept. A revision gets a new date under the old one.
A seller’s work is that seller’s opinion, published under their name, and is not advice unless marked so by a licensed professional on file. The telephone and the exact position of a seller are never published. Nothing here alleges that anyone broke any law.
